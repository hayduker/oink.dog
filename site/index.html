About Me

I am a systems software engineer with a decade of experience building the underlying engines, runtimes, and developer tooling that power complex data processing systems. My engineering philosophy centers on determinism, memory safety, performance optimization, and pursuing programming as a craft focused on modularity and simplicity.

In 2026, I stepped away from my long-term role to take a planned personal and technical sabbatical. Alongside completing 450 miles of wilderness thru-hiking on the Arizona Trail and other travel, I've dedicated this time to diving deep into modern systems programming in Rust, exploring decentralized systems and compiler design.

I am looking to bring my background in resource-efficient systems to teams building compilers, language tooling, or local-first, decentralized systems—environments where low-overhead execution, predictability, and memory safety are valued as primary features.

nVoq Experience

Over nearly a decade at nVoq, a healthcare-focused speech recognition startup, I grew from an R&D contractor into a Technical Lead. I wore many hats across the engineering stack: starting in machine learning and computer vision research, moving on to architect the company's next-generation high-throughput dictation servers, and eventually building core developer tooling and containerized AWS build pipelines that made our work more efficient.

Personal Projects

Outside of my professional work, my coding is driven by a deep curiosity for how systems work under the hood. Over the years, this has evolved from building interactive music tools and DSP software towards a focus on compilers and distributed systems.

Recently, I have been building whipsnake—an ahead-of-time compiler that targets WebAssembly for a subset of Python—and developing local-first, peer-to-peer media transfer tools using Rust and Iroh. I also actively contribute to the open-source ecosystem, particularly working with CRDTs, P2P networking tools like Iroh, and decentralized data layers like Automerge and Holochain.
